Research Areas

Vision & Language

Multimodal models combining vision and natural language understanding for advanced AI applications.

ML & DL

Development of novel algorithms and architectures for supervised, unsupervised, and reinforcement learning, model merging, and PEFT.

Medical Imaging

Deep learning applications for medical image analysis and clinical decision support.

Computer Vision

Advanced algorithms for image and video analysis, object recognition, and scene understanding.

IoT & Embedded Sensors

Design and deployment of IoT systems and embedded sensors for data collection and analysis.

Robotics & HRI

Human-robot interaction and autonomous systems for real-world applications.

How to Reach AImageLab

By Plane

The nearest airport is the Airport of Bologna Guglielmo Marconi.

The airport of Bologna has very frequent direct connections to all major European Hubs (Milan, Rome, Frankfurt, Brussels, Paris, London, Amsterdam).

Shuttle Service to Modena:

Departure times: 6:15, 8:15, 9:15, 10:15, 12:15, 16:15, 18:15, 21:15

Duration: About 1 hour

Cost: Approximately €10 (10% discount available for return tickets)

Ticket: Purchase aboard the shuttle

By Train

Modena has direct train connections to/from:

  • Milan
  • Zurich
  • Frankfurt
  • Paris

Visit www.raileurope.com to plan your journey.

From the railway station: Take bus 7N or 9 to "GOTTARDI"

By Car

Take the A1 Motorway and exit at "Modena Sud".

After the toll booth, turn right at the junction onto Via Vignolese.

Drive straight for approximately 10 km until you reach a junction with traffic lights.

Cross the road and turn right into Via Gelmini.

You will see the Faculty buildings ahead on your left.
