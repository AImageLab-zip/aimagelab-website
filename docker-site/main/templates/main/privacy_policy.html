Welcome to AImageLab

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information on this website. We are committed to transparency regarding data collection and usage.

No Personal Data Collection

This Site is designed to provide information only. We do not collect, process, or store any personal information from users. This means:

  • We do not request or store names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any other personal identifiers.
  • No user accounts or logins are required to view or access content.
  • We do not collect user-submitted data of any kind through forms, comments, uploads, or messages.

No Cookies or Tracking Technology

This Site does not use cookies or similar tracking technologies for analytics, advertising, personalization, or any other purpose.

  • No session cookies, persistent cookies, third-party cookies, or tracking pixels are placed on users' devices.
  • We do not use Google Analytics, Matomo, or any other user-tracking or behavior-monitoring tools.
  • As a result, your visit to this Site is inherently anonymous.

Log Files

Although we do not intentionally collect personal information, standard technical logs may be generated automatically by the web server (e.g., IP address, browser type, access time, pages viewed). These logs are generated for system maintenance and security purposes only, and are not used to identify individual users or to track user behavior.

Third-Party Content

The Site may contain links to third-party websites or external resources. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external sites. Please review the privacy policies of any site you visit from links on our Site.

Children's Privacy

This Site is not directed at children under the age of 13. Because we do not collect personal information, we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, law, or Site features. The "Last Updated" date at the top will indicate the most recent revision.

We recommend that you review this page periodically.
